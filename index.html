❓ FAQ

Is it really free? Any limits?

Yes — free, no sign-up. The engine is community-run, so if one server is busy SnapVault automatically tries another.

Why did my link fail?

Private, login-only, or deleted posts can't be fetched — only public content works. Age-restricted YouTube videos may also fail.

Does TikTok keep its watermark?

No — TikTok downloads come watermark-free by default.

Where do my files go?

Straight to your device's normal download folder. Your history stays in this browser only.

Do I need to install anything?

No. SnapVault is a web-based video downloader — it runs entirely in your browser on phone, tablet, or desktop, so there's nothing to install and no extensions needed.
